# Identity — decision record (SB-25-02)

**Recorded:** 2026-06-10.

## What an account is for

Two things only: a stable identity across devices for correspondence
matches (challenges address *someone*), and ladder integrity (results
attach to an identity that cannot be casually impersonated). Nothing
else. Accountless play remains first-class forever.

## The mechanism: anonymous device keys

An account **is a keypair** (ECDSA P-256, generated by WebCrypto in the
player's browser). The public key is the identity; the account id is
its SHA-256 fingerprint. Registration uploads the public key and a
display name — nothing else exists to upload. Every mutating request
(rename, delete, and later: challenge acceptance, result attestation)
is signed by the private key; the service verifies and never holds any
secret.

- **No email. No password. No OAuth.** There is nothing to phish,
  leak, or sell, and nothing to demand from a player.
- **Cross-device transfer** = export/import the key (the same
  philosophy as the game data: yours, portable, never ours).
- **No recovery.** A lost key is a lost account — recorded plainly.
  The profile and matches stay on the device; a new account is one
  click. (Passkey/WebAuthn upgrade is the recorded path if recovery
  ever becomes worth its complexity.)

## Stored schema — exhaustive

| Field | Why |
|---|---|
| `accountId` | SHA-256 fingerprint of the public key (the identity). |
| `publicKey` | JWK, to verify signatures. |
| `name` | Display name (game-font sanitized, like local profiles). |
| `createdAtIso` | Operational hygiene. |

Nothing else is stored, and the service **rejects requests carrying
unexpected fields** — data minimization as a contract test, not a
policy document.

## The service

A zero-dependency Node HTTP service (`services/identity/`), storage in
a single JSON file — deliberately small enough to self-host anywhere.
Deployment is the maintainer's activation step (the SB-20-01 Pages
precedent); CI proves the contract against a local instance.

## Deletion

`DELETE`, signed. Removes the record entirely and verifiably (a
follow-up GET is 404). Local data is untouched — it was never the
service's.
